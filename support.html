Support

Need help? Email steadyhelm@icloud.com. Please include your iPhone model, the iOS version and, if you can, a screenshot of the problem.

The full instructions are inside the app: open Menu → Instructions.

Common problems

No alert arrived. Check Alert me by: on that alert. Check that Low Power Mode is off and that SteadyHelm was not swiped away. For Listing Alert on its own, check that SteadyHelm was left open on screen. For Telegram, send a test message from the Telegram screen.

Readings look wrong. Tap Zero again while the boat is sitting normally.

Telegram setup did not work. Tap Clear Telegram Settings and start again from step 1.

To keep SteadyHelm watching

  • Do not swipe SteadyHelm away in the app switcher.
  • Turn off Low Power Mode while an alert is on. iOS can pause SteadyHelm to save power.
  • For Anchor Watch, set Location to Always for SteadyHelm in the iPhone Settings app.
  • If the phone restarts, unlock it and open SteadyHelm. Your alerts carry on with the same settings.
  • If SteadyHelm stops watching for any reason, this phone shows a notification within about 10 minutes. Allow notifications when SteadyHelm asks.

Get alerts on another phone

Telegram can send SteadyHelm's alerts to the phone you carry. Set it up in Menu → Alert Methods → Telegram. The screen walks you through six steps.

Leaving a spare phone on the boat? Set up Telegram on your own phone first. Then tap Show QR on your phone and Scan QR on the spare phone to copy the settings across. The spare phone does not need Telegram.
